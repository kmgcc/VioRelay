# VioRelay

让 iPhone 成为 Mac 的无线麦克风。

VioRelay 在 Apple 设备之间传递低延迟的高质量音频——打开 iPhone 上的 App，Mac 就能立刻用它录音、通话、直播。无需额外硬件，扫一次码，以后自动连接。

# VioRelay (English)

Use your iPhone as a wireless microphone for your Mac.

VioRelay streams low-latency, high-quality audio across your Apple devices. Open the app on your iPhone, and your Mac can use it right away — for calls, recording, streaming, anything that needs a good microphone. No extra hardware. Scan once, and it connects automatically from then on.
